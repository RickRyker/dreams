# Guilds Documentation

**Guilds** are social organizations that allow players to organize and share resources.

## Management
- **Creation**: Requires a minimum level (default: 5, configurable in world configuration).
- **Recruitment**: New members must be at least level 5 (configurable in world configuration).
- **Ranks**: A hierarchy from 0 to 9. Ranks possess a structured permission set defining access to invitations, kicks, promotions, rank management, and the treasury.

## Treasury & Assets
- **Donations**: Players can donate non-bound items to the guild's treasury.
- **Tagging**: Players can "tag" their items with the guild's identity.
- **Shared Storage**: Players earn guild credits by donating items or performing actions, which can be spent according to the guild rules.

## Audit Trails
- **Membership Logs**: Records of every join, leave, promotion, and kick.
- **Treasury Logs**: Records of item tags, untags, and credit transactions to prevent theft and mismanagement.

## Example Guild Rank Structures
- **Classic Medieval / Military Style**: Recruit, Initiate, Footman, Sergeant, Knight, Captain, Commander, Marshal, High Marshal, Grand Master
  - **Other Options**: Squire, Warden, Champion, General, Lord Commander
- **Fantasy / Arcane Guild Ranks**: Novice, Acolyte, Adept, Disciple, Enchanter, Spellblade, Archon, Magister, High Magus, Grand Master
  - **Other Options**: Seer, Oracle, Arcanist, Sage, Hierophant
- **MMO‑Style Heroic Progression**: Recruit, Initiate, Adventurer, Veteran, Elite, Champion, Hero, Paragon, Legend, Grand Master
  - **Other Options**: Vanguard, Sentinel, Mythic, Ascendant, Exemplar
- **Noble / Feudal Hierarchy**: Commoner, Page, Squire, Knight, Baron, Viscount, Earl, Duke, Prince, Grand Master
  - **Other options**: Regent, Chancellor, High Lord, Sovereign
- **Dark / Rogue‑Themed Ranks**: Outsider, Initiate, Cutpurse, Shadow, Stalker, Nightblade, Whisper, Phantom, Dreadmaster, Grand Master
  - **Other options**: Shade, Reaper, Blackguard, Wraith
- **Explorer / Ranger / Wilderness Guild**: Greenhorn, Trailhand, Pathfinder, Scout, Ranger, Huntsman, Trailmaster, Warden, High Warden, Grand Master
  - **Other options**: Wayfinder, Wildkeeper, Beastmaster

## Overview

The Regional Cuisine System ties food culture to geography, climate, and the people who inhabit each region. Every region features:

- **Unique local ingredients** influenced by environment and trade.
- **Signature dishes** that reflect the history and identity of the people.
- **Cooking bonuses** granted when dishes are prepared using regional ingredients and methods.
- **Reputation tiers** that unlock new recipes, vendors, and world content as a cook's renown grows.
- **Integration hooks** for quests, factions, economy, and world events.

Cuisine is not merely sustenance — it is diplomacy, history, and identity on a plate.

## Regions

---

### Northern Highlands

> *"The mountains do not give freely. Every meal is earned."*

**Climate:** Cold, alpine, prone to heavy snowfall and short growing seasons.  
**Culture:** Stoic, self-sufficient clans who value endurance and hospitality.  
**Culinary Identity:** Hearty, slow-cooked, smoke-cured, and fermented foods built for cold survival.

#### Local Ingredients

| Ingredient | Type | Rarity | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ironbarley | Grain | Common | Grows at high altitude; dense and nutty flavor |
| Snowroot | Vegetable | Common | Starchy tuber; staple of clan cooking |
| Glacierfish | Protein | Uncommon | Cold-water fish; firm flesh, mild taste |
| Crag-Boar Loin | Protein | Uncommon | Tough but rich; requires long braising |
| Ashleaf Herb | Herb | Rare | Smoked herb found near geothermal vents |
| Frostberry | Fruit | Rare | Tart, high-sugar berry; ripens in late autumn |
| Alpine Honey | Sweetener | Uncommon | Produced by cold-adapted hives; bold floral notes |
| Grey Salt | Mineral | Common | Mined from highland seams; coarser than sea salt |

#### Cooking Methods

- **Slow braising** in iron pots over hearthfire
- **Cold smoking** over ashwood or pine
- **Salt-curing** and **fermentation** for long-term preservation
- **Stone baking** using heated flat rocks

### Coastal Shores

> *"The sea feeds all who dare to ask."*

**Climate:** Temperate maritime; mild winters, sea breezes, high humidity.  
**Culture:** Seafaring traders and fisherfolk; open, adaptable, with rich trade networks.  
**Culinary Identity:** Fresh, bright, and layered flavors from the sea and the vibrant spice trade.

#### Local Ingredients

| Ingredient | Type | Rarity | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tidewrack Kelp | Vegetable | Common | Briny sea vegetable; used fresh or dried |
| Silverscale Herring | Protein | Common | Small, oil-rich fish; staple of the coast |
| Brineprawn | Protein | Common | Sweet shellfish; eaten boiled or raw |
| Deepwater Crab | Protein | Uncommon | Dense claw meat; used in stews and pies |
| Sea Fennel | Herb | Common | Anise-like herb growing on cliffsides |
| Saffron Thread | Spice | Rare | Traded from inland; adds color and floral depth |
| Citrus Rind | Flavoring | Uncommon | Used as zest; preserves are common |
| Pearl Oyster | Protein | Rare | Delicate bivalve; prized for ceremonial dishes |

#### Cooking Methods

- **Grilling** over driftwood or charcoal
- **Steaming** in seawater with aromatic herbs
- **Pickling** in brine and citrus
- **Raw preparation** (ceviches, crudo)

### Desert Expanse

> *"Spice is the shadow of the sun — it burns, then warms."*

**Climate:** Arid, extreme temperature swings between day and night.  
**Culture:** Nomadic and semi-nomadic tribes; masters of preservation and long-haul cooking.  
**Culinary Identity:** Intensely spiced, slow-cooked, oil-rich dishes designed for preservation and energy.

#### Local Ingredients

| Ingredient | Type | Rarity | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sundried Dates | Fruit | Common | Sweet, dense; eaten raw or pressed into paste |
| Desert Lentil | Legume | Common | Protein-rich; basis of many stews |
| Sandspice | Spice | Common | Sharp, earthy; made from dried desert pepper |
| Camel's Milk | Dairy | Uncommon | Slightly salty; fermented into yogurt and cheese |
| Scorpion Tail | Protein | Rare | Eaten fried; considered a delicacy and a test of courage |
| Mirewort | Herb | Uncommon | Bitter desert plant; aids digestion |
| Amber Resin | Flavoring | Rare | Extracted from desert trees; used for smoking and finishing |
| Obsidian Salt | Mineral | Uncommon | Volcanic salt from ancient lakebed; extremely fine |

#### Cooking Methods

- **Clay-pot slow cooking** buried in embers
- **Dry spice rubs** before open-fire roasting
- **Sun-drying** for preservation
- **Flatbread baking** on hot stones

> *"From the soil springs everything. Waste nothing."*

**Climate:** Temperate continental; four distinct seasons, fertile plains.  
**Culture:** Agricultural communities and market towns; practical, community-focused.  
**Culinary Identity:** Abundant, seasonal, farm-to-table cooking with rich dairy and grain traditions.

#### Local Ingredients

| Ingredient | Type | Rarity | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Heartland Wheat | Grain | Common | Fine-milled flour; basis of bread and pastries |
| Field Mushroom | Vegetable | Common | Earthy; grown in shaded meadows |
| Farmstead Cheese | Dairy | Common | Aged hard cheese; nutty and crumbly |

| Wildflower Honey | Sweetener | Common | Lighter than alpine varieties; floral and sweet |
| Thyme & Sage | Herb | Common | Grown in kitchen gardens; aromatic |
| Redcap Radish | Vegetable | Common | Peppery root; eaten raw or pickled |

### Frozen Tundra

> *"The land gives little. You must honor every piece."*

**Climate:** Subarctic; perpetual permafrost, extreme winters, brief summers.  
**Culture:** Small insular communities; deeply spiritual relationship with food and animals.  
**Culinary Identity:** Nutrient-dense, animal-centric, whole-beast cooking with minimal waste philosophy.

#### Local Ingredients

| Ingredient | Type | Rarity | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tundra Caribou | Protein | Common | Leaner than cattle; all parts used |
| Permafrost Moss | Vegetable | Common | Foraged; used as a thickening agent in broths |
| Ice Seal | Protein | Uncommon | Rich, fatty meat; critical caloric source |
| Snowbell Mushroom | Vegetable | Uncommon | Grows beneath snow in late spring |
| Whale Fat (Blubber) | Fat | Rare | Rendered for cooking oil; distinctive flavor |
| Lichenwort | Herb | Common | Astringent herb; aids preservation when brewed |
| Frozen Cloudberry | Fruit | Rare | Vitamin-rich; thawed and eaten in small quantities |
| Bone Marrow | Fat/Protein | Common | Roasted and spread; rich, gelatinous |

#### Cooking Methods

- **Freeze-preservation** (natural permafrost caches)
- **Boiling in bone broth** for extended periods
- **Fermentation** in skin pouches
- **Rendering fat** for oil and heat

### Jungle Canopy

> *"Every leaf hides a flavor. Every root, a secret."*

**Climate:** Tropical rainforest; year-round heat, heavy rainfall, exceptional biodiversity.  
**Culture:** Decentralized village networks; deep ecological knowledge and shamanistic traditions.  
**Culinary Identity:** Vibrant, fermented, bitter-sweet, and aromatic; emphasizes layering of complex flavors.

#### Local Ingredients

| Ingredient | Type | Rarity | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Heartpod Fruit | Fruit | Common | Sweet-tart tropical fruit; eaten fresh or fermented |
| Jungle Cassava | Starch | Common | Starchy root; processed to remove toxins before use |
| Tree Pepper | Spice | Common | Fruity heat; less sharp than highland varieties |
| Canopy Beetle Larva | Protein | Uncommon | Rich in fat; roasted or eaten raw |
| Mudfish | Protein | Common | River fish; smoked or fermented as paste |
| Bloodvine Leaf | Herb | Rare | Red-veined leaf; used for wrapping and mild anesthetic flavor |
| Darkspore Mushroom | Vegetable | Rare | Fermented black mushroom; intense umami depth |
| Cacao Pod | Sweetener | Uncommon | Ground into bitter paste or fermented for beverages |

#### Cooking Methods

- **Leaf-wrapping** and steaming in banana or bloodvine leaves
- **Open-pit roasting** in earthen ovens
- **Fermentation** of fruits, proteins, and grains
- **Paste grinding** on stone mortars

**Climate:** Tropical volcanic islands; geothermal activity, rich mineral soils.  
**Culture:** Island confederacies tied by sea-lanes; ceremonial feasting culture.  
**Culinary Identity:** Mineral-rich, earth-cooked, communal feast food with strong ceremonial significance.

#### Local Ingredients

| Ingredient | Type | Rarity | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lava Taro | Starch | Common | Grown in mineral-rich volcanic soil; earthy flavor |
| Archipelago Tuna | Protein | Common | Large open-water fish; eaten raw, smoked, or grilled |
| Ember Chili | Spice | Common | Fire-roasted before use; deep, smoky heat |
| Cocoanut | Fat/Fruit | Common | Milk and flesh used in nearly every dish |
| Sea Urchin Roe | Protein | Uncommon | Rich, briny delicacy; eaten fresh |
| Black Sesame | Seed | Uncommon | Toasted; used as garnish and in pastes |
| Geothermal Salt | Mineral | Rare | Crystallized from natural geothermal springs; prized |
| Firebloom Flower | Herb | Rare | Edible flower from volcanic slopes; sweet and smoky |

#### Cooking Methods

- **Imu (earth oven)** — food wrapped and slow-cooked underground with heated stones
- **Sashimi-style raw preparation**
- **Open-fire grilling** on black-rock grates
- **Coconut-milk braising**

> *"The horse carried us. The horse fed us. The horse is family."*

**Climate:** Temperate grassland; wide open plains, windy, seasonal extremes.  
**Culture:** Horse-mounted nomadic clans; warrior societies with strong guest-right traditions.  
**Culinary Identity:** Fermented dairy, grilled meat, and portable provisions — food designed for people always on the move.

#### Local Ingredients

| Ingredient | Type | Rarity | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Steppe Horse Meat | Protein | Common | Lean, gamey; eaten at celebrations or in extremity |
| Kumiss Base (Mare's Milk) | Dairy | Common | Fermented into a mildly alcoholic beverage |
| Wild Onion | Vegetable | Common | Foraged; pungent, used in nearly every savory dish |
| Steppe Millet | Grain | Common | Drought-resistant grain; ground into flat cakes |
| Dried Mutton | Protein | Common | Jerky-style preservation; essential trail food |
| Blackthorn Berry | Fruit | Uncommon | Astringent; cooked into sauces or dried |
| Saltstick Root | Mineral/Vegetable | Uncommon | Naturally salty root; used as a salt substitute |

#### Cooking Methods

- **Skewer grilling** over open grassfire
- **Fermentation** in leather flasks (kumiss, dried curds)

- **Boiling in felt-insulated pots** (steam retained by wrapping)

## Cooking Bonuses

Dishes grant bonuses when consumed. Bonuses scale with **ingredient quality**, **regional authenticity** (using native ingredients), and **cook's reputation tier**.

### Bonus Categories

| Category | Description |
|---|---|
| **Vitality** | Increases maximum health or health regeneration |
| **Endurance** | Reduces stamina drain; increases stamina recovery rate |
| **Focus** | Improves concentration, precision skills, or mana-related stats |
| **Warmth** | Resistance to cold environments and weather conditions |
| **Heat Resistance** | Resistance to fire and extreme heat environments |
| **Fortitude** | Damage reduction; resistance to debuffs |
| **Speed** | Movement speed and reflex bonuses |
| **Charisma** | Improved trade prices; faction reputation gain |
| **Recovery** | Shortens status effect durations; aids healing |
| **Luck** | Increases rare drop chance; critical success probability |

### Bonus Scaling Rules

- **Pristine ingredients**: All bonus values ×1.5
- **Full regional authenticity** (all ingredients from native region): Additional +15% to all bonuses
- **Tier 4+ reputation in dish's region**: Bonus duration +50%
- **Stacking**: Only one dish bonus per category may be active at a time. The stronger value overrides.
- **Legendary variants**: Dishes crafted with Legendary-rarity ingredients may activate a **Mythic Bonus** — a unique, powerful fourth effect not listed in standard tables.

## Reputation Tiers

Each region tracks the cook's renown independently. Reputation is earned through:

- Cooking and serving regional dishes
- Completing region-specific culinary quests
- Donating food to communities, festivals, or factions
- Winning cooking competitions
- Discovering and documenting lost regional recipes

### Tier Definitions

| Tier | Title | Rep Required | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Outsider** | 0 | Basic vendor access; common ingredients only |
| 1 | **Curious Traveler** | 100 | Uncommon ingredients at vendors; Tier 1 recipes revealed |
| 2 | **Known Cook** | 300 | Regional cooking station access; side-quest invitations |
| 3 | **Respected Guest** | 700 | Rare ingredients appear at vendors; hidden recipes unlocked |
| 4 | **Honored Chef** | 1,500 | Extended bonus durations; faction discounts; mentor NPCs |
| 5 | **Legendary Culinarian** | 3,000 | Legendary ingredient sources revealed; ceremonial feast access; unique cosmetic rewards |

### Reputation Decay

- Reputation does not decay passively.
- Certain actions can reduce regional reputation: selling substandard food to locals, refusing community feast invitations, or working with hostile factions.
- Reputation loss is capped at −200 per incident.

### Region-Specific Titles

Each region grants a unique honorific title upon reaching Tier 5:

## Integration Notes

### Quest & Narrative Hooks

- **Regional recipe discovery**: Lost or forgotten recipes can be found through archaeology, elder NPCs, or dungeon exploration. Recovering them grants large reputation bonuses and often triggers a cutscene or lore entry.
- **Feast events**: Major world events (seasonal festivals, military campaigns, coronations) require a cook to prepare a feast. The quality of the feast influences the outcome of the attached quest.
- **Faction diplomacy**: Serving a rival faction's preferred dishes can shift faction relations — a powerful soft-diplomacy mechanic.
- **Poisoning and sabotage**: Rare narrative missions may require a cook to tamper with a dish. These are flagged as morally consequential choices.

### Economy Integration

- Regional ingredients are affected by **supply and demand**. Over-farming an area causes local ingredient vendors to raise prices or stock to deplete.
- **Trade caravans** appear seasonally, carrying cross-regional ingredients. Players can invest in or protect caravans for economic bonuses.
- **Black market ingredients**: Some rare items are regulated by factions. Purchasing them risks reputation loss with authorities but may be necessary for certain recipes.

### Skill Tree Integration

The regional cuisine system connects to the **Culinary Skill Tree**:

- `Forager`: Increases ingredient yield and quality when gathering in the wild.
- `Regional Expert`: Unlocks passive bonus for authenticity when cooking in a native region.
- `Preserver`: Extends the shelf-life of cooked dishes before they expire.
- `Feast Master`: Enables multi-serving preparation — one cook feeds an entire party.
- `Spice Alchemist`: Allows substitution of one spice for another without losing authenticity bonus.
- `Trade Tongue`: Improves access to cross-regional ingredients at vendors.
- `Mythic Hand`: Unlocks the chance to craft Mythic Bonus dishes with Legendary ingredients.

### Weather & Environmental Modifiers

- Cooking outdoors in adverse weather (blizzard, sandstorm, heavy rain) applies a **−10% quality penalty** unless the appropriate regional station is available.
- Certain dishes grant **environmental condition bonuses** that are only relevant in their native region's biome (e.g., Marrow Broth's Warmth bonus only activates in cold zones).

### Multiplayer & Co-op Considerations

- In multiplayer, feast dishes can apply bonuses to the whole party if cooked at a **Tier 3+ regional station**.
- A dedicated **Cook role** in party composition provides passive ingredient quality upgrades to all dishes prepared in that session.
- Regional reputation is **per-character**, not shared — each player must build their own standing.
